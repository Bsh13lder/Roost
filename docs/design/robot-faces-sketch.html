Boxbot, screen faces

Boxbot keeps its boxy head, rivets and antenna, with no face. Its dark screen shows live signals instead: what the bot is doing right now. Three screen styles (S1 to S3), each shown on six head shapes, Roost's five statuses (Resting, On it, Asking you, Stuck, Off duty), three extra moments (Thinking, New message, Done) and six colours. The signals move. Tap a style and tell Claude, e.g. "S1 with the Wide shape".

Signals are drawn in the bot's colour, lightened to glow on the dark screen. The main bot wears the star.
