Roost avatars, round five: Ghosts

Ghosts only, Chick and Penguin are dropped. The eyes are the face now: two big round lenses with a strong highlight, nearly half the width of the head, and they still read at 24 px. Everything else is motion: a float with a shadow that scales, a hem that waves, blinks and glances, and a quiet, physical animation for each thing that happens to a bot. Move your pointer and every ghost on the page looks your way; move it fast and the nearest one does a double-take. The app does not change until this round is picked.

What changed, and what we stay away from

Eyes first

Two round ink lenses, together 42 to 46 % of the head's width, with a big top-left highlight and a small lower glint that slide against the pupil. No mouth. Feeling comes from the lids (wide, narrow, lines, closed arcs), the gaze and the body.

Cool, not anime

No sweat drops, no sparkle eyes, no blush, no ^^ squint, no cartoon sparks. Smooth easing with weight, light instead of symbols: a glow that breathes, a ring that expands, an orbiting dot, a scan line, a brief glitch, a z made of light.

One family, ten silhouettes

Dome, Tall, Wide, Round, Droopy, Spire, Comet, Cape, Puff and Hooded are one path function with a few numbers each, so they morph into each other. All keep the translucent edge glow in the bot's colour and a soft inner gradient.

Small stays still

Under 32 px only blinks remain. prefers-reduced-motion and the Calm robots setting leave only blinks everywhere. One shared frame loop moves the eyes, paused when the tab is hidden or a bot is off screen.

Playground pick · then press an animation

Pick a shape (press and drag across the tiles to watch the ghost morph), a colour and a status, then press any animation. The big ghost and its four neighbours each follow your pointer on their own. Hover one to make it lean in, click to poke it. Random life plays the idle variety by itself.

A · The family shapes · colours · statuses

Ten silhouettes in one colour at the picker's 56 px, six colours at 96, 40 and 24 px, the five statuses, the main bot's star and an uploaded picture inside the ghost.

B · In context sidebar · hero · chat · picker

The cozy-kit pieces the ghosts live in: the sidebar's pinned tiles (3 per row, 64 px, with names and the main bot's job), the Profile's panel hero, the chat header pill, and the avatar card with its Robot / Upload / Reset tabs.

C · Performance and accessibility

One shared requestAnimationFrame loop owns the pointer and every live ghost: it reads the pointer once per frame, keeps each bot's box in a cache that scroll and resize invalidate, and only touches bots an IntersectionObserver says are on screen; those off screen also get their CSS animations paused. It stops when the tab is hidden, when nothing is on screen, and when motion is turned down. Per frame it writes a handful of CSS variables (--ex, --ey for the eyes, --lx, --lr for the lean); every other motion is a CSS animation on a class or an attribute, including the hem, which interpolates the skin's d between three paths kept in CSS variables.

Every light effect (the ring, the orbit, the scan line, the "?", the z's) is aria-hidden; the whole bot is one role="img" with its name and status. prefers-reduced-motion turns everything off except slow blinks. The same switch is a setting:

Calm robotsBots keep still: no eye following, no float, no events. They only blink, slowly.

D · How this maps to code

Everything stays SVG plus CSS animations, with no new dependency. A ghost is one stateful SVG: data-s for its status, data-face for its lids, a class for an event in flight. The status loops, the event motions and the lid shapes are all CSS rules on those attributes, so a status change is an attribute flip, not a redraw. The only JavaScript that runs per frame is the small shared pointer loop.

web/src/components/robot/shapes/ghost.ts

The ten silhouettes as a table of a few numbers each (width, dome, side, lift, taper, sag, tent, puff, hem kind, waves, depth, eye line) and one pure ghostPath(shape, wave, phase) that samples 64 points (7 a side, 30 over the top, 20 along the hem) and returns the smooth path. Every shape has the same 64 points, so d interpolates for the hem wave and the picker's morph. It returns d0, d1, d2, the box, the eye line and radius.

RobotFace.tsx · eyes.tsx · fx.tsx

RobotFace keeps its props (shape, color, moment, star, picture, pictureEyes, size, still, events) and draws the halo, the skin with its edge glow, the rim, the hood, the eyes and the light effects inside the four groups the motion needs: pose, float, evt, lean. eyes.tsx draws one eye as lid → blink → gaze → pupil (ink lens, two highlights) plus the closed arc. fx.tsx draws the ring, the orbit, the scan line, the "?", the z's, the typing dots and the RGB split, all hidden until a class shows them.

robot.css

The classes and keyframes above, scoped by html:not(.calm) and .robot--live. The per-bot numbers travel as CSS variables on the SVG: --d0/--d1/--d2 (the hem's three paths), --oy (the body's centre), --sh (its height), --blink-dur/--blink-delay/--idle-delay (so a team never blinks in step). Ink, halo and star tokens join tokens.css.

useLookAt.ts · lookAt.ts

useLookAt(ref, { eyeY, enabled }) registers the SVG with one module-level loop that owns the pointer (position, speed), the box cache, the IntersectionObserver and the visibilitychange pause. It writes --ex, --ey, --lx, --lr, toggles robot--offscreen, and fires the pointer micro-events (double-take on a fast pass, look-around when the pointer leaves the window, the glances and the random idle life). Disabled below 32 px, when still is set, and while the lids are not open.

Statuses and events

Live bot_status sets data-s: Resting → rest, On it → onit, Asking you → ask, Stuck → stuck, Off duty → off. Tool activities become events (keyed, transient): a create_* result → created; a run that ends in a reply → done; a new message in the chat → msg; the first streamed thinking of a run → think; a Stuck card → stuck once, then the status; the bot's first mount in a view → hello, its unmount → bye. A click is poke, handled inside the component.

look.ts

Shape ids become ghost names. Old saved shapes keep their stored value and draw through the table below (the six V1 heads and the five S1.6 birds); roost/constants.py, api/types.ts and tests/test_bot_look.py move in step. The picker's drag morph is a 450 ms tween of the 64 points in the component, with the hem animation paused by robot--morph while it runs.
