A sketch for the next look: five original robot directions to choose from (A), the kit made smaller and cozier, shown next to today's (B), and one recommendation with the code it touches (C). Nothing in the app changes until a direction is picked.

A · Five avatar directions pick one

Every direction is drawn on a 48-unit grid so it stays sharp at 24px. Bodies are one flat colour with a gentle darker lower band and a small highlight. The five statuses show in the face and the pose, and three of them add a small corner badge so they still read at chip size. The main bot wears a butter star on the lower corner. A picture you upload sits in the screen or face area, and the status badge stays on.

C · Recommendation

Go with direction 1, Soft bot, plus the cozy kit. It is the calmest of the five, and it is still plainly a robot and plainly Roost's. The squircle head and pill visor read cleanly at 24px. The visor is a ready frame for an uploaded picture, and the round eyes carry the five statuses without new glyph art. It also keeps today's idea of a head with a screen, so moving to it feels like an upgrade, not a rebrand. Pebble is the strongest runner-up if you want even less detail. Sprout's leaf could join later as one more Soft bot antenna. Ship the kit tweaks first, because they are small and low-risk and change how every menu feels. Then swap the heads.

web/src/components/robot/heads.tsx

Five Soft bot heads on a 48-unit grid (Square, Tall, Wide, Dome, Eared), each drawn by one two-tone body helper (base colour, a lower band at shade 0.74 and a small highlight) and a visor box. Head names reuse classic, tall, wide and soft and add eared.

web/src/components/robot/look.ts

The old shapes keep drawing through the one table: cube → Square and monitor → Wide join LEGACY_SHAPE_TO_HEAD next to the five bird shapes. Stored avatars keep their values and nothing migrates. roost/constants.py (ROBOT_HEADS, LEGACY_AVATAR_SHAPES) and tests/test_bot_look.py move in step.

signals*.tsx, StatusBadge.tsx, RobotFace.tsx

The three screen styles give way to eyes in the visor (closed, looking, looking up, crossed, flat). StatusBadge becomes the one corner badge (? · ! · moon) for every robot, with or without a picture. robotScreen() still accepts stored screen values.

web/src/components/robot/palette.ts

Ten new swatches. BOT_DEFAULT_COLORS and roost/constants.py stay the same list. Any stored hex still draws.

web/src/styles/robot.css

Breathe, look, shake and the antenna pulse, all inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference and off when still is set. Posture (tilt, settling down, dimming) stays put without motion.

web/src/styles/tokens.css

The token table above. web/tests/tokens.test.ts still holds every text colour at 4.5:1 or more.

web/src/ui/ + ui-overlays.css, ui.css

Menu, Select, Tabs, Popover, Sheet, Tooltip and Toast take the new tokens. Highlighted items use --color-hover in place of the accent fill. Menu and Select items get a 16px icon slot. Surfaces swap the 1px border for --edge-inner and the layered shadow.
