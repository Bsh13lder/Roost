Roost avatars, round three

Softer, rounder, no antennas. Five families of 12 shapes each (A), the best two placed in the cozy kit from round two (B), and one recommendation that maps onto web/src/components/robot/ (C). The app does not change until a direction is picked.

What changed, and what we stay away from

The feedback asked for the same soft, squishy charm as a well-known competitor's avatars, but in a look that is clearly Roost's own. Every family below follows these rules.

Symmetric, named shapes

Not free-form, irregular blobs. Every outline is built from an egg, a stone, a cut gem or a pillow on one 48-unit grid, mirrored left and right and sitting on one shared baseline.

Our own face language

Not paired vertical pill eyes, and never a tilted eye pair. We use round dots with a white highlight, a beak notch, ^ ^ arcs, one visor slit, or a small screen inset. The eyes stay level, and only the whole head tilts.

The roost is the hook

Roost's first avatars were birds. The Chick and Hatchling families bring them back: an egg, a tuft, a crest, a wing, a shell. They are perched on a soft shadow and act like birds: a curious head tilt, a tuck to sleep.

No antenna, no extra parts

The statuses use only the eyes and the posture. The two exceptions you asked for are the "?" glint (Asking you) and the "z" (Off duty), and both sit beside the head.

Resting: a slow blink On it: eyes look around, a gentle bounce Asking you: head tilt and a ? glint Stuck: X or dizzy eyes and a small wobble Off duty: closed eyes, settled and dimmed, a z

Motion runs only when your system allows it (prefers-reduced-motion). Without motion, each status still reads from the eye shape, the tilt, the dimming and the glint.

A · Five families pick one

Each family has 12 shapes, so a team of bots can all look different, and every shape takes any of the ten colours from round two. Bodies have a soft light from the upper left, a small gloss and a ground shadow. That gives the squishy, touchable feel without outlines.

B · The best two in the cozy kit hero · sidebar · picker

The same three pieces for each: the Profile's panel hero, the sidebar with its pinned row, and the avatar picker card with its Robot / Upload / Reset tabs, the shape grid and the colour dots.

C · Recommendation

Go with Chick. It has the same soft, squeezable warmth you liked, but it comes from Roost's own story: a roost is where birds rest, and Roost's first avatars were birds. It is distinct from the competitor in all three places their look lives. The silhouettes are named, mirrored bird forms (egg, plump, tufted, crest, wing, puff) that sit on one perch line, not free-form, lopsided blobs. The face is two small, close-set round eyes, each with a white highlight, often over a tiny beak notch. There are no vertical pills, and the eye pair never tilts. The behaviour is a bird's: a curious head cock with a "?" glint when it asks you something, and a tuck and a "z" when it goes off duty. At 24px a Chick is a coloured egg with two dots, which is the most readable of the five. Mochi is the runner-up if you want something rounder and less bird-like. Two or three Hatchling shapes (shell, cap) could later join Chick's family as extra shapes.

web/src/components/robot/heads.tsx

The six box heads are replaced by twelve Chick shapes as data: each is a few numbers (width, height, squareness, egg taper, flat base) plus an optional tuft, crest or wing. One pure chickPath() turns them into a mirrored path on the 48-unit grid. The shapes are drawn once and are easy to test.

web/src/components/robot/look.ts

ROBOT_HEADS becomes the twelve Chick names. The bird shapes from S1.6 (round, egg, pear, plump, crest) draw as themselves again. tall and wide keep their names, and LEGACY_SHAPE_TO_HEAD now maps the other four box heads (classic, soft, cube, monitor) instead (table below). Stored avatars keep their value and nothing migrates. roost/constants.py (ROBOT_HEADS, LEGACY_AVATAR_SHAPES), api/types.ts and tests/test_bot_look.py move in step.

RobotFace.tsx · new eyes.tsx

RobotFace draws the body (soft radial light, gloss, ground shadow), the face and the star. A new eyes.tsx holds the five eye states (blink, look, up, X, closed) and the beak. An uploaded picture clips to the body, with a rim in the bot's colour.

signals*.tsx · StatusBadge.tsx · moments.ts

The three screen styles (glow, pixel, wave) retire. robotScreen() still accepts stored values, and drawing ignores them. StatusBadge becomes the "?" and "z" glints. STATUS_MOMENT keeps feeding the face. The extra moments (think, msg, done) reuse look, up and blink.

palette.ts · robot.css · tokens.css

Round two's ten colours, plus a beak colour and a ground-shadow token. Blink, look, bounce, wobble, glint and zed run only under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference, and are off when still is set.
